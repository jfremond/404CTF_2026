# Canular Savant (3/3)

## Objective
For this challenge we have to find the member of `Nicolas Bourbaki` who recently
"celebreated" his birthday. A hint given to us is that he's not part of this world
anymore.

## Resolution

We go on the wikipedia page for [Nicolas Bourbaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Bourbaki) and we check which member was born around may (date of release
of the challenge).

One member was born in may and is now dead. That member is
[André Weil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9_Weil).

The flag for this challenge is `404CTF{andre-weil}`
