# Papioutai

## Objective
Oh my, the ENS—that school that has produced so many brilliant minds!

A friend would like you to help her find information about her great-great-grandfather,
Jean Roux, a former resident of Ulm who died for France in World War I in a place whose
name ends in “demanges.”

Can you find Jean Roux’s graduation year, as well as his city of birth?

## Resolution

We search on the national database for [people who died for france in World War I](https://www.memoiredeshommes.defense.gouv.fr/conflits-et-operations-2/premiere-guerre-mondiale/morts-pour-la-france-de-la-premiere-guerre-mondiale/faire-une-recherche?arko_default_670f920646a08--ficheFocus=).

We focus on people named `Jean Roux` and look for the one who died in a place whose
name ends in "demanges".
We find the correct [Jean Roux](https://www.memoiredeshommes.defense.gouv.fr/ark:40699/m005239ff9660d6c.moteur=arko_default_670f920646a08). We see that he was born in
`Saint-Estèphe` he engaged for the army in `1913` and died in `1914` so he must have
graduated from the ENS before.

We look for a Jean Roux in the [UNS directory](https://www.archicubes.ens.fr/).
We find a Jean Roux that graduated in `1912` so we have all the elements to capture this
flag.

The flag for this challenge is: `404CTF{1912_saint-estephe}`
