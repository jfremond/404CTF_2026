# Canular Savant (2/3)

## Objective
For this challenge we have to find the name of a group of French scientists.
The name of this group is heard in a song of a famous alternative music group from
Colombus.

## Resolution

We first need to find the name of this alternative band from Colombus.
A simple internet search let's us know the band is `Twenty One Pilots`.

We then need to find the French scientists group they're mentioning in their
song.
Another simple internet search shows us the group they're singing about is
`Nicolas Bourbaki` in their song `Morph`.

The flag for this challenge is `404CTF{nicolas-bourbaki}`
